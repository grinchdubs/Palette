___How do I Import Belka into my Project?___
Just dumb the .tox file into your .toe and process the output as liked. It might be a good idea to restart your .toe after the import.

___How to prepare VideoFiles___

On the COMP-level you can define a folder-path. Please organize your Videoclips in subfolders as they will 
not be recognized by Belka when just inside the defined folder.
It is best to convert your 
clips to HAP-Codec to guarantee best performance.
Every Clip will be played in a time given by the beat. Your clips should not be too long. I
 found 5 seconds very suitable
If you bring new files inside Belka, before starting to Jam, press the Create Preview-Pulse on the BelkaCOMP.
 


___How to prepare FX___

Working with FX .tox-Files is the same as with the videofile-folder. Define the folder and load the desired .tox into the subfolders.
They only need one In- and one OutTOP. 
At the moment, there is no way to control the FX from the interface.

___How to load Clips and FX___
Press the Load-Button. You got an overview of the clips inside the selected folder. Use the arrows to 
switch folders. Use Drag and Drop to load the Videofiles onto the clips.



___How to play Clips and FX___

Click the Clip or FX to activate. Selecting an active clip will disable it. 

___How to edit FX-Parameter___
Select the FX-Button in the Topbar. Click the "Select-Button" and choose the effect you want to edit. 
The 4 Sliders in the control-panel will change accordingly.


___How to set the Timing___

The BPM-Button in the top row can be used to Tap to Sync. Only 4 Taps in a row will be taken.

___How to Autoplay___
To autoplay use the 5 red buttons in the top area. You can deactivate autoplay for different layers ind the controll-section.

___How to manipulate a layer___

Press “Controll” on tthe right side of a layer. On the right you can now change the playback-speed and the composition method.




___Changing the Interface___

To change the number of Layers, Clips per Layer or FX per Layer, use the custom parameter on the Comp-Level.

If you want to change the appearance you can change the interface dimensions inside the BelkaCOMP. 
The W and H sector are values between 
0 and 1 and define the space taken by the main Window in percentage of the Belka comp. 
The interface will automatically
change to the given parameters. 
It is also possible the change the color of FX, Edit and Clip-Buttons. 
The “ClipButtons” value will also change the 
appearance of the Speed, Load and Settings Button.
